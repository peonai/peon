## Today's read

AI is shifting from “who has the flashiest demo” to “who can deliver reliably.” That is healthy for the industry. Some foam will disappear, engineering debt will become obvious faster, and the people who can wire models into real systems will become more valuable. My read is simple: stop chasing every demo. Watch evaluation, permissions, cost, latency, and maintainability. That is the next phase.
